# Ad Headline Examples (Reference)

40 famous ad headlines, 1980s to now, grouped by the seven families in `mode-3-ad-headlines.md`. Use for reference and calibration, not to copy.

Wording and years are from memory, except four checked against 2024–25 sources: Apple "1984", Wendy's, Got Milk?, Snickers. Least certain years: Specsavers, The Economist, Marines, Be All You Can Be, Oatly.

## 1. Commands that give permission
- Just Do It (Nike, 1988)
- Think Different (Apple, 1997)
- Be All You Can Be (U.S. Army, ~1980)
- Obey Your Thirst (Sprite, 1994)
- Taste the Rainbow (Skittles, 1994)
- Share a Coke (Coca-Cola, 2011)
- Stay thirsty, my friends (Dos Equis, 2006)

## 2. Questions that open a gap
- Where's the beef? (Wendy's, 1984)
- Got milk? (California Milk Processor Board, 1993)
- Can you hear me now? (Verizon, 2002)
- What's in your wallet? (Capital One, 2000)

## 3. Concrete promises
- When it absolutely, positively has to be there overnight (FedEx, 1982)
- 1,000 songs in your pocket (Apple iPod, 2001)
- Red Bull gives you wings (1987)
- Intel Inside (1991)
- 15 minutes could save you 15% or more on car insurance (GEICO, 1990s)

## 4. Flips and paradoxes
- Reassuringly expensive (Stella Artois, 1982)
- "I never read The Economist." — Management trainee, aged 42 (The Economist, late 1980s)
- Don't Buy This Jacket (Patagonia, 2011)
- Impossible Is Nothing (Adidas, 2004)
- It's like milk but made for humans (Oatly, 2014)
- Priceless (Mastercard, 1997)
- Why 1984 won't be like 1984 (Apple, 1984)

## 5. Identity and belonging
- The Few. The Proud. The Marines. (U.S. Marine Corps, ~1980)
- The Best a Man Can Get (Gillette, 1989)
- Like a Girl (Always, 2014)
- Real Beauty (Dove, 2004)
- Believe in something. Even if it means sacrificing everything. (Nike, 2018)
- I'm lovin' it (McDonald's, 2003)
- Belong Anywhere (Airbnb, 2014)

## 6. Wit and self-awareness
- You're not you when you're hungry (Snickers, 2010)
- The man your man could smell like (Old Spice, 2010)
- Thanks, 2016. It's been weird. (Spotify, 2016)
- Should've gone to Specsavers (Specsavers, early 2000s)
- Our blades are f***ing great (Dollar Shave Club, 2012)
- Maybe she's born with it. Maybe it's Maybelline. (Maybelline, 1991)

## 7. Fear and consequence
- This is your brain on drugs. (Partnership for a Drug-Free America, 1987)
- Don't mess with Texas (Texas anti-litter campaign, 1985)
- Friends don't let friends drive drunk (Ad Council, 1983)
- Dumb Ways to Die (Metro Trains Melbourne, 2012)

## Verified notes
- Apple "1984": Chiat/Day, aired Jan 22, 1984, Super Bowl XVIII, directed by Ridley Scott. Closing line: "And you'll see why 1984 won't be like 1984." (9to5Mac, Jan 2025)
- Wendy's: aired Jan 10, 1984; agency Dancer Fitzgerald Sample. One outlet reports sales up 31% that year. (The Takeout, Feb 2025)
- Got Milk?: from a 1993 focus group where people went without milk for a week. Goodby, Silverstein & Partners. Only a modest sales bump. (Chowhound, Dec 2024)
- Snickers: BBDO, launched 2010. First-year global sales up 15.9%, share up in 56 of 58 markets. (Fortune, Sep 2025)
