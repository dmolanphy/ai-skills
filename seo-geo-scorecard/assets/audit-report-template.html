The Finding — Read This First

GEO readiness determines who controls the message. On a specific, decision-relevant question, a well-optimized site gets cited as the source of truth. A blocked or poorly-structured site doesn't disappear — the AI finds someone else's description of it instead, and cites that. You don't stop being findable. You stop being the one who gets to describe yourself.

What This Audit Does and Doesn't Measure
  • Measures: technical readiness — whether AI and search engines can access, parse, and extract this site's content.
  • Does not measure: live AI-citation share — how often a brand is actually mentioned in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini answers. That requires paid tools with API access at scale (Profound, Gumshoe, Semrush AI visibility).
  • ToS restrictions vs. technical blocks are different levers. [Which sites have which, and what the live engines actually did in each case.]
  • Known limitation: a compliant robots.txt is not proof of actual access — CDN/firewall-level blocking (e.g., Cloudflare's AI Bot Management) can override it entirely before a bot's request ever reaches the server.
